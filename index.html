Selected Projects

Bio-Inspired Dental Composites Simulation
Abaqus Python MATLAB

Designing functionally graded interfaces between ceramic and polymer layers to reduce interfacial stresses and enhance the durability of dental restorations, inspired by the natural dento-enamel junction between the enamel and dentin in human teeth.

Developing a Stress Fracture Risk Prediction Tool
Abaqus ScanIP Matlab Python

Developing an athlete-specific tibial stress fracture risk prediction pipeline that combines baseline CT-derived geometry, image-based finite element simulations, and a trained machine learning model to quickly estimate stress/strain at common fracture sites.
