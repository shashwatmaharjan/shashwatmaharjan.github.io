About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Villanova University. My research focuses on two areas: using materials design and finite element analysis to improve dental composites, and applying biomechanics, finite element analysis, and machine learning to understand and prevent musculoskeletal stress fractures.

My background spans computational modeling, structural health monitoring, and geothermal energy research. Before joining Villanova, I earned my Master’s and Bachelor’s degrees in Mechanical Engineering from Central Michigan University. I am especially interested in developing data-driven and physics-based tools that improve prediction, reduce experimentation time, and support innovations in injury prevention, healthcare, and bio-inspired material design.
